## Key Notes
 
- **Headquarters:** Burlington Tower, Business Bay, Dubai, UAE
- **Markets:** UAE (primary) and Libya (food, pharma manufacturing base)
- **Al Sanidan Furniture Showroom** is a standalone direct subsidiary — not grouped under any sector division
- **Arc Auto Spare Parts** and **Almutakbal Alsheeh Ltd** are both under the Spare Parts division — independent from Aviation
- **Midline Trend Building Contracting LLC** is the head company of the Construction division, with 4 subsidiaries beneath it
- **Majestic Elite Properties** appears under Construction in this structure
